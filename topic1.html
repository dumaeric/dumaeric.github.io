State Surveillance

A Hypothetical Scenario

You’re at the restaurant with your friends, talking about your cute but fat cat. The consensus is that something needs to be done. Your real-life Garfield needs to be put on a diet. You are back at your place, Garfield meows for food (again), and you open your phone. What a coincidence! Instagram shows you an advertisement for diet cat food.

But what if this is not a coincidence at all? What if your phone could listen to your onversations and monetize that information? What if every online and offline activity can be translated into data so somebody or something can get better at selling you something? This is not a hypothetical situation. This is the era of surveillance capitalism.
